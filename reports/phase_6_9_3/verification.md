# Phase 6.9.3 Exness XAUUSD broker specification verification

**Contract acceptance blocked.** Two read-only runs on 18 November 2026 at 16:40 and 16:42 EAT each returned 144 P/L and 24 margin calculations. The final run used the same pinned Exness KE demo identity throughout and ended with a successful metadata recheck.

- XAUUSD reported three digits, point and tick size 0.001, contract size 100, and generic, profit and loss tick values of USD 0.10 per lot. Volume was 0.01–200 lots in 0.01 increments. Calculation mode 0 was Forex. Base and margin currencies were XAU, with profit and account currencies USD.
- In each run, 138 of 144 P/L rows met both the price-move and tick-value formulas within half a cent plus floating-point slack. Six one-tick rows returned ±USD 0.01 for expected magnitudes of USD 0.001 or 0.002. Those discrepancies exceeded the tolerance by USD 0.004 or 0.003, respectively, after allowing half a cent (before floating-point slack). The rounding behavior remains unverified.
- In the final run, all 24 margin rows were positive, linear in size at each side and price, and consistent with `lots × contract size ÷ leverage` in XAU multiplied by the probe entry in USD per XAU, using an effective margin rate of one. Maximum absolute residual was USD 0.004. This margin conversion finding covers the sampled account and prices. MT5's Forex definition includes a broker margin-rate factor. [MQL5 margin calculations](https://www.mql5.com/en/book/automation/symbols/symbols_margin)
- The offline risk path accepts XAU margin only when the scenario explicitly identifies USD conversion and supplies the verified scenario's USD margin per lot. The test models USD 0.20 spread in the quote, USD 0.03 entry and USD 0.10 stop slippage per unit, USD 5 round-trip commission per lot and USD 3,782.49 margin per lot. These costs are illustrative inputs. They are not broker cost measurements. The test checks a 0.10-lot plan with USD 14.25 modeled stop loss and USD 378.249 modeled margin, and checks currency, positive margin input and free margin guards.

The MetaQuotes Phase 5.2 rejection and its existing USD diagnostic rules remain in force. The Exness result stays rejected under the half-cent acceptance rule. Broker cost schedules, historical applicability, historical execution and profitability, prospective holdout collection and evaluation, and automated demo or live trading remain unverified or blocked by the existing gates. No risk scenario was certified using the rejected snapshot.
