## 1. Exness KE Limited — CMA 162

Kenya materials cover resident clients, advertise gold on MT5 and promote MT5 demo. Group demo help lists Standard, Pro, Raw Spread and Zero, with Standard Cent excluded. The Kenya tick archive describes Pro XAUUSD and Standard XAUUSDm. Confirm actual demo entitlement and suffix. XAUUSD247 has its own schedule and is outside this proposal. The 13 November 2026 group contract page lists XAUUSD commission of USD 3.50 Raw Spread and USD 5.50 Zero per lot per side. It also lists group high-margin rates of 0.1%–0.5% and zero hedged margin. Actual Kenyan demo applicability is unverified. The accessible Kenyan commodity table returned no numeric rows. The local account ceiling is 1:400; margin can change with instrument, equity and market conditions. Gold swaps vary, usually with triple Wednesday and possible holiday adjustments. Exact demo swap points, mode, fees and margin tiers are open. Get confirmation that the exact XAUUSD stream is available on the Kenyan MT5 demo and that data reads count toward the stated 30-day inactivity rule. Confirm gold-specific swap, demo commission, spread distribution, min/step and effective margin. The help gives GMT+2/GMT+3 and US DST to align with New York close, but lists transition examples through 2022 and describes gold hours and rollover using MT4 labels. Obtain dated MT5 demo evidence and Python history coverage. ## 3. Pepperstone Markets Kenya Limited — CMA 128

Its Kenya framework describes a demo and the group demo terms include the Kenyan entity. The group fee page publishes Razor Spot Gold commission from USD 3.50 per lot per side, dependent on currency/size and subject to MT4/5 micro-lot rounding. Group hours state GMT+2/GMT+3 with US DST. Group demo page describes 60 days for MT5. Verify these for the Kenyan demo, including exact symbol, gold contract size, minimum and step, actual spread, swap/rollover, commission, leverage/margin and tick/bar depth.
