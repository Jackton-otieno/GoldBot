## Verification rules

- Resolve quote and trade independently for an exact server and symbol.
  Supply broker-attested half-open UTC intervals including applicable
  exceptions. A cross-midnight session is one continuous UTC interval.
  Supply explicit closed evidence for a known holiday, and leave an
  uncovered time unknown. Overlapping assertions fail verification.
- Supply a contemporaneous independently verified UTC uncertainty
  bracket and server-specific verified offset windows. An effective
  interval and a single unambiguous conversion must cover the entire
  bracket. Freshness also requires usable bid/ask and a uniquely decoded
  tick within the verified open quote interval, at or before the
  earliest possible now and at most 60 seconds old at the latest
  possible now.
- Risk-day evidence also requires an exact account and policy identity
  and provenance from the policy authority. Local, UTC and server
  midnight and broker swap posting have no implicit reset semantics.
  A reset requires a dated, direct successor to the recorded day and
  positive equity attested at its exact opening boundary for the same
  scope. Repeats preserve the current day's losses and latch. Missing,
  conflicting, expired, skipped or ambiguous evidence and missing
  opening equity preserve the supplied state.

The new evaluators take caller-reviewed evidence. The issuer and
reference fields identify evidence for review. They have no external
authentication or calendar discovery and no bundled production
schedule. A caller must bind observations to their actual source and
persist an accepted risk transition atomically with its ledger. The
evaluators are standalone. Existing market reads, risk gates,
portfolio replay, and trading entry points are outside their call path.
