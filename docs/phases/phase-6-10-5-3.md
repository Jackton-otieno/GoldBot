## Acquisition and validation

Raw new bytes are isolated under `historical_data/phase_6_10_5_3`. The
manifest distinguishes acquired files, references to earlier immutable
reports and failed requests. An HTTP success and a local SHA-256 establish
retrieval and integrity for the inspected bytes. Provider documents and
identity checks supply the scope and interpretation. They do not establish
complete trading coverage.
[Manifest](../../reports/phase_6_10_5_3/data-manifest.json) ·
[Source evidence](../../reports/phase_6_10_5_3/source-evidence.md)

- The Dukascopy tool retrieves a single public XAU/USD hour and audits a
  pinned BI5 file with byte, decompression and row limits. The daily archive
  is a different format variant and is outside this implementation.
- The HistData tool retrieves at most one monthly Generic ASCII tick ZIP with
  explicit compressed and member size caps. It scans source timestamps in
  order, streams the compressed member and audits one selected UTC hour. It
  uses fixed UTC−05:00 rather than New York civil time.
- The Exness tool uses the existing verified installation, encrypted opt-in
  and independent journal pin. It requests at most 256 records at each of up
  to two raw anchors and preserves the returned API fields. Raw labels
  receive no UTC conversion and are never used to claim UTC coverage.

The UTC sample aggregator uses half-open M1, M15 and H1 bins. It keeps
source order, reports shared timestamps and repeated payloads, validates
prices and spread, and generates independent bid and ask OHLC. The close
availability label is the end of the bin. Later ticks cannot change an
earlier closed bin. Empty bins stay absent and are counted. Intertick
silence and source edges are reported without assuming a market closure.
These are observed quotes rather than fills or executable prices.
This phase verified UTC conversion and aggregation on synthetic fixtures.
The real archived sample was checked in its original raw coordinates.

## Reproduction

Use the GoldBot Python environment from the project root. The public
download commands require a fresh destination. The offline audit requires
the recorded digest and selected hour from the manifest.

~~~powershell
$env:OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS = '1'
$env:OMP_NUM_THREADS = '1'
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B -m tools.histdata_sample --file <zip> --hour <utc-hour> --sha256 <digest>
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B -m tools.dukascopy_sample audit --file <bi5> --hour <utc-hour> --sha256 <digest>
& .\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -B -m unittest discover -v
~~~
