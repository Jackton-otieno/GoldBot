3741.006. At minimum volume they place the opening notional just below, at
and just above the half-cent boundary. The grid spans 0.01, 0.02 and 0.07
lots, 1, 4, 5, 6, 9 and 37 ticks, both sides and both price directions.

repeat contract probes and the expanded precision grid. The exact formula

to the API's numeric input types only at the calculator call.

the Exness KE USD demo ending 9498 on ExnessKE-MT5Trial9. The local pin
matched the independently recorded account and installation. On terminal
build 6324 with Python package 5.0.6231, all 216 independent calculator
results matched the frozen model. The 840 first-pass results from the
exploratory grid also matched and are reported separately as prior evidence.
Only 12 of the independent outputs matched the unrounded formula exactly.
The absolute difference exceeded USD 0.00500001 in 44 cases. This
compares the new grid to the existing tolerance without rerunning or
changing the acceptance validator.

## Risk implications

Among 400 adverse archived inputs, the calculator returned zero 47 times
and understated exact linear loss 112 times, by at most USD 0.009. It
overstated loss 128 times, by at most USD 0.008. Across the 108 adverse
independent inputs, the calculator and frozen model understated exact loss
50 times and overstated it 52 times, by at most USD 0.009 in either
direction. The calculator returned zero for 23 of those adverse moves.
The difference between two half-up notional errors is strictly
less than one cent because each positive notional's rounding error lies
in the half-open interval (−0.005, +0.005].

The existing risk manager caps spend by per-trade, remaining daily-loss and
drawdown limits. The sizer floors volume to the broker grid using modeled
stop distance, slippage and commission. In an isolated sizing example,
0.01 lots fits a remaining USD 0.001 budget at a one-tick exit distance.
For a BUY from 3741.005 to 3741.004, the frozen hypothesis predicts a USD
0.01 loss, USD 0.009 above that budget. This demonstrates a possible
rounding-only breach at a sizing boundary under the hypothetical model.
Actual broker booking, fees, slippage and price gaps have separate effects.
The model is unsuitable for reducing a loss estimate or bypassing the
existing validation and risk gates.
