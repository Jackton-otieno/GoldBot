1 November 2026, including close-only before opening and a one-hour seasonal
shift. The public holiday calendar lists Thanksgiving and Christmas dates.

observed quotes, close-only permission, and permission to open new trades
are distinct. Production session evidence needs dated account/server-specific
quote and trade intervals and applicable holidays, maintenance and exceptional
