## Risk feasibility

The research grid applies the existing executable-side stop geometry to
each available replay ATR at a fixed hypothetical USD 3,800 midpoint.
It uses the more demanding of the two valid BUY and SELL minimum-lot
losses. Each loss includes rounded entry-to-stop distance, adverse stop
slippage and round-trip commission. The spread affects executable
quotes and admission. The separately reported immediate round-trip
friction includes the full spread and both adverse slips and is kept
separate from the planner's stop-loss calculation.

Each threshold is modeled minimum-lot stop loss divided by 0.005 and
rounded up to an equity cent. Percentiles use nearest rank. These are
fresh-budget thresholds for a flat, unencumbered account. The available
budget can be lower once the daily loss and drawdown caps bind. Tail fills,
changing spreads, actual funding costs and booked rounding remain unverified.

## Margin, rejected entries and profitability

At 0.01 lot and the illustrative price, 0.25%, 1% and 5% entry-margin
assumptions require USD 9.50, 38 and 190 of free margin. The frozen
research replay instead used USD 2 at minimum lot. USD 3,000 passes
all these minimum-lot entry-margin screens if it is entirely free,
while USD 100 fails the hypothetical 5% screen. None is a verified
maintenance-margin or stop-out model.

At baseline costs, the original USD 3,000 replay rejected 94 of 132 M15
attempts and 115 of 127 H1 attempts for minimum volume. Of those
rejections, 76 M15 and 14 H1 would fit a fresh USD 15 budget at the same
simulated entry inputs. The actual path-dependent budget remained
binding. Rejection status is the first reason observed. The report
retains all four costs and the frozen strategy-comparison counts.
